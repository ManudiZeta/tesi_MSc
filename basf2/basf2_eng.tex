\section{The Belle II Analysis Software Framework}
To perform the simulations and the analyses of this thesis, the Belle II Analysis Software Framework (\textbf{basf2}) has been employed. It is the primary software used in all aspects of the online data-processing chain at Belle II, such as generating simulated data, unpacking raw data, performing reconstruction (tracking, clustering), and carrying out high-level analysis. The final analysis steps, the offline Analysis (histogramming, fitting distributions, etc.) are usually performed in other frameworks, such as \textbf{ROOT}.
